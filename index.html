Meta ads, January 2027 round: copy

Five ads that send people to the free VSL (about 25 min). The ads sell the video, not the workshop: no price, no dates, no ticket.

  • Videos follow the best freebie format from November ("Ad 1 – New Video", CPL 16.5): Agita talking straight to camera in the studio, paint tubes in hand, paintings behind her, 30–40 seconds, one take, no B-roll. All three can be filmed in one session.
  • Photos follow the two best image formats: the Før/Etter split ("Image 1 Before After", webinar) and the polaroid collage ("Image 2 – Du maler", direct sale).
  • Button on every ad: Learn more ("Se mer"). Destination: the VSL page.
  • Claims come from the VSL script (copy/vsl-jan-2027/vsl-manus.md). The warm/cold colour rule is not stated anywhere in the ads, because Agita has not confirmed it yet.
